When severe rodent breaches occur, our team provides prompt dispatch to deploy targeted mechanical traps and seal primary exterior entry voids. ## How to Prepare Your Property for a Service Visit

To ensure your technician can conduct the most thorough inspection and execute treatments safely and efficiently, we recommend taking a few simple preparatory steps prior to arrival:

1. **Clear Access to Critical Utility and Inspection Points:** Ensure that technicians have unobstructed access to your electrical panel, attic access hatch (often located in hallway closets or garages), crawlspace access doors, and water heater closets. Move stored cardboard boxes or clothing racks away from these openings.
2. **Secure Domestic Pets:** While our technicians practice clean-tech protocols and use targeted formulations, curious dogs and cats can become agitated or inadvertently step on treatment zones before drying. Please keep household pets in a secure, untreated room or in a pet carrier during the service visit.
3. **Note Specific Sighting Locations:** Take photos of any visible insects, droppings, or wood frass if safe to do so. Note the time of day when scratching noises or insect activity are most pronounced to help the technician pinpoint harborage nests faster.
4. **Unlock Exterior Gates:** Ensure that side gates leading to backyards, perimeter fences, and garden areas are unlocked so the technician can perform a complete 360-degree perimeter assessment.
