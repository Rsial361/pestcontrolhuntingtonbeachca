## How We Use Collected Information

Huntington Beach Pest Control collects and uses User personal information for the following specific business purposes:

1. **To Coordinate Field Service and Technician Dispatch:** The information you provide allows our local coordinators to route qualified, background-vetted pest control technicians to your physical property for diagnostic evaluation.
2. **To Provide Itemized Project Estimates:** We use property dimensions, symptom notes, and contact details to generate clear written estimates and communicate treatment scopes.
3. **To Improve Customer Service:** Information provided helps us respond more efficiently to customer service requests, emergency dispatch inquiries, and support needs.
4. **To Facilitate Direct Telephone Contact:** When you submit an inquiry, you authorize our dispatch coordinators or assigned service contractors to contact you via telephone call or SMS text message regarding your pest management request.
5. **To Comply With California Legal Mandates:** We maintain records of customer inquiries and dispatch verifications in accordance with California business transparency and consumer protection regulations.

## California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Rights

If you are a resident of California, you possess specific statutory rights regarding your personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to Know:** You have the right to request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you over the past 12 months.
- **Right to Delete:** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information that we have collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will never discriminate against you, deny you services, or charge you different rates because you exercise any of your CCPA privacy rights.
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Because we do not sell personal data to third-party data brokers, an opt-out mechanism for data sales is not applicable; however, you may request that your dispatch records be removed from our active routing database at any time.
